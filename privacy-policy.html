Bloom & Sort – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains how Bloom & Sort (Android package: com.bloomandsort.game) handles information. The app is provided by Artic Pixel Studio, an independent developer in Finland. Contact for privacy questions: bloomsort.games@gmail.com.

1. Information stored on your device

The game stores your progress, scores, settings, virtual rewards, optional player name, avatar and local clan information on your device. This allows you to resume playing and customize the game. The current game does not require an online account or send these game records to a developer-operated game server. Local clan features are not an online multiplayer service.

2. Advertising and consent

The app uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) for interstitial and optional rewarded ads, and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage privacy choices. Google’s advertising SDK can collect and share information such as IP addresses (which may be used to estimate general location), device and advertising identifiers, app interactions, ad interactions and diagnostic information, including performance and crash information. These data are used for advertising, measurement, analytics and preventing fraud. Google and applicable advertising partners may receive or process this information. Depending on your location, settings and consent, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

Where required, a consent form is shown before eligible ad requests. You can review or withdraw consent via the Ad privacy option in the game settings when applicable. Refusing consent does not prevent you from playing the game, although advertising availability may change.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, and Google ad settings.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Local data are used to provide game functionality and remember your preferences. Where European data protection law applies, we rely on providing the requested game features, consent where required for advertising and related storage or access, and legitimate interests in security, fraud prevention and responding to support requests where permitted. Google and applicable partners may process data under their own applicable privacy terms and legal bases.

4. Sharing, international transfers and security

Advertising-related information may be processed by Google and applicable advertising partners and transferred outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area. Such providers describe their safeguards and transfer mechanisms in their privacy policies. We do not operate a separate game database. We use the platform’s local storage and the advertising SDK’s security mechanisms; no storage or transmission method is completely secure.

5. Retention and deletion

Local game records remain on your device until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall it, subject to Android backup and restore settings. Clearing them may also erase your game progress. Google and advertising partners retain information according to their own privacy policies and applicable obligations. You may contact us to request access to or deletion of personal data we directly hold. Support communications are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to handle the request or meet legal obligations.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, to receive a portable copy, and to withdraw consent without affecting prior lawful processing. Contact us at the email above. You may also complain to your local data protection authority; in Finland, see the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman. You can also manage advertising identifiers and ad preferences in Android settings.

7. Children and privacy

Children’s information requires special protection. Please do not enter personal contact details in the optional player-name or clan-name fields. If you believe a child’s personal information has been processed inappropriately, contact us. Advertising and privacy controls must be configured for the app’s declared audience.

8. Contact and changes

For questions or requests about this policy, email bloomsort.games@gmail.com. We will update this page when the app’s privacy practices change.
